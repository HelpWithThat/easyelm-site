Phone Mouse by Easy Elm help

Pair your phone with your computer

  1. Make sure Bluetooth is on, on both your phone and your computer.
  2. Open Phone Mouse by Easy Elm on your phone and tap Make phone discoverable. Your phone stays visible for 2 minutes.
  3. On your Windows computer, open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Add device > Bluetooth.
  4. Pick your phone from the list.
  5. Check that the code is the same on both screens, then confirm on both.

Next time, just open the app. It connects to your computer by itself. If not, tap your computer’s name in the app.

Windows tip: something stopped working

If something stops working after a Windows update (for example, the volume buttons), remove and re-pair:

  1. On your computer, open Settings > Bluetooth & devices.
  2. Find your phone, open its menu (…), and choose Remove device.
  3. Pair again using the steps above.

Cancel a monthly plan

Monthly Pro plans are handled by Google Play. To cancel:

  1. Open the Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile picture at the top right.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  4. Choose Phone Mouse by Easy Elm and tap Cancel subscription.

Store Reminder by Easy Elm help

Add a reminder

  1. In Google Maps, find the place and tap Share.
  2. Choose Store Reminder by Easy Elm.
  3. Add what you need there, like milk, stamps, or a prescription pickup.

You get a reminder when you arrive, or even when you’re just driving by.

Lists that come back

Set a list to reset every day, on the days you pick, every few weeks or months, or whenever you tap Reset.

Phone Intercom by Easy Elm help

Get started

  1. Put the app on each phone and give each one a name.
  2. If the phones are on the same Wi-Fi, they can find each other on their own. They can also talk from different places if each phone has internet (Wi-Fi or mobile data).
  3. Hold someone’s button to talk, and let go to stop. Or hold Everyone.

Can’t see another phone?

If you expected the phones to find each other on their own, check that they are on the same Wi-Fi. If you connected them with a code or a username, each phone needs internet (Wi-Fi or mobile data), and Phone Intercom by Easy Elm should show Ready.
