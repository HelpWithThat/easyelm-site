Privacy

Easy Elm apps are made to respect your privacy. We don’t collect, sell, or share your personal data, and our apps have no ads. Each app’s policy is below.

  • Easy Elm Remote (page on its own)
  • Proximity Reminders (page on its own)

Easy Elm Remote Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Easy Elm Remote turns your Android phone or tablet into a Bluetooth mouse, keyboard, mic and volume control for your computer. Easy Elm Remote is made by Easy Elm. Easy Elm does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • The app sends nothing about you, your typing, or your computer to Easy Elm.
  • The app’s own code makes no network calls.
  • What you type and say is never stored.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Pro purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. Easy Elm does not see or store your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether you own Pro, and remembers the answer on your phone so Pro works offline. The 7-day free Pro trial runs on your phone and needs no sign-up or card.

The app has no internet permission of its own. The Google Play Billing Library it uses adds internet and network-state permissions so it can talk to Google Play and send its own diagnostic logs to Google. Google handles that information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions and why they’re needed

  • Bluetooth (connect / advertise): to pair with and connect to your own computer, and to send the mouse movements and keys you press. These go straight to your paired computer over Bluetooth.
  • Microphone: only while you tap the mic button for voice typing. The app passes the audio to your phone’s built-in speech recognition service (for example, Google’s), which turns it into text. Easy Elm Remote does not record, save, or send audio itself. Voice typing uses Google speech recognition and may need an internet connection. The speech service handles audio under its own privacy policy.
  • Notifications / foreground service: shows the “connected” notification so the Bluetooth link stays on while the screen is off, and reminds you before your free Pro week ends.
  • Vibration: a small buzz when you press keys or use gestures.
  • Wake lock: keeps the connection working while the app is in use.

Data stored on your phone

The app keeps your settings (theme, sizes, saved computer names and Bluetooth addresses), your Pro status, and the free-trial dates only in its private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. App backups are turned off.

Children

Easy Elm collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.

Proximity Reminders Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Proximity Reminders is made by Easy Elm. It reminds you of things when you arrive at, or drive by, places you choose. Easy Elm does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data.

Your location

The app uses your phone’s location only on your phone, to know when you are near a place you saved, so it can show your reminder. Your location is never sent to Easy Elm or anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking.

Places you share from Google Maps

When you share a place from Google Maps into the app, the app opens that Google Maps link to find the place’s address and map spot. This is the only time it uses the internet. Your own location is not part of it.

Permissions and why they’re needed

  • Location (including in the background): to notice when you arrive at or pass a saved place, even when the app is closed.
  • Notifications: to show your reminders.
  • Start at boot / battery settings: so reminders keep working after your phone restarts.
  • Internet: only to open Google Maps links you share into the app.

Data stored on your phone

Your places, lists and settings are kept on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If you have turned on Android’s own phone backup, Android may include them in your backup with Google.

Children

Easy Elm collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? We’d love to hear from you. support@easyelm.com

Easy Elm Intercom Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Easy Elm Intercom is a push-to-talk intercom for the phones in your home or workplace. It is made by Easy Elm. Easy Elm does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data.

Your voice

When you hold a button to talk, your voice goes straight from your phone to the other phones over your local Wi-Fi. It is encrypted between your phones. It does not go to Easy Elm or over the internet, and it is not saved.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No internet needed. It works over your own Wi-Fi.
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking.

Permissions and why they’re needed

  • Microphone: only while you hold a button to talk.
  • Wi-Fi and network: to find the other phones on your Wi-Fi and talk to them.
  • Notifications / foreground service: so you can hear people calling you even when your screen is off.
  • Vibration and wake lock: to alert you and keep the intercom ready.

Data stored on your phone

The name you give your phone and your settings are stored only on your phone. Other phones on your Wi-Fi see your phone’s name so they can call you. Uninstalling the app deletes them. App backups are turned off.

Children

Easy Elm collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes
