Proximity Reminders Coming soon

Never forget the thing you went out for.

What it does

Location reminders that go off when you drive by or arrive at favorite places.

You get home from the store and realize you forgot the one thing you needed. Proximity Reminders fixes that by reminding you at the right place, right when it matters.

Some lists come back again and again. Set a list to reset every day, on the days you pick, every few weeks or months, or whenever you tap Reset.

How it works, in 3 steps

  1. Share any place from Google Maps straight into the app.
  2. Add what you need there, like milk, stamps, or a prescription pickup.
  3. Get a reminder when you arrive, or even when you’re just driving by.

Who it’s for

Designed to be easy for all ages, with big, easy buttons that are clear at a glance and 12 color themes to make it yours. Great for:

  • Grocery and hardware store runs
  • Picking up prescriptions
  • Dropping off mail or returns
  • Anything you keep forgetting on the way
