Become a tester

Testers get the full app free while it’s in testing.

Step 1: Join the tester group

Tap the button below. You’ll see a page called Easy Elm Remote - Testers. Tap the blue Join group button.

Join the tester group

Use the same Google account that’s on your Android phone.

Step 2: Become a tester

Tap the button below, then tap Become a tester on that page.

Become a tester

If it says “App not available,” make sure you’re signed in to that same Google account and joined the group in Step 1, then wait a few minutes and try again.

Step 3: Install the app

On that same page, tap the download it on Google Play link, or tap the button below. Then tap Install.

Install Easy Elm Remote

Step 4: Stay in for 14 days

Keep the app installed and open it now and then. Tell us what you think at support@easyelm.com.
