Become a tester

Testers get the full app free while it’s in testing. It takes about two minutes.

Use the same Google account for every step, the one on your Android phone. If you’re signed in to more than one Google account, Google can quietly switch to a different one, so it’s easiest to do these steps on your phone.

Step 1: Join the tester group

Tap the button below. You’ll see a page called Phone Mouse by Easy Elm - Testers. Tap the blue Join group button near the top.

A box pops up that says Join Phone Mouse by Easy Elm - Testers. You don’t need to change anything. Tap Join group again in the bottom corner of the box. You’ll see “Successfully joined the group.”

Join the tester group

Step 2: Accept the invite

Tap the button below. Google Play says You’re invited to test Phone Mouse by Easy Elm. Tap the blue Accept invite button.

Become a tester

Seeing “App not available”? Don’t worry. If you just joined the group, give it a few minutes and try again. If it still doesn’t work, Google is probably using a different Google account than the one that joined the group. Open the link on your Android phone, or switch to the right account, and try again. If you run into any problems, let us know at support@easyelm.com so we can fix it.

Step 3: Install the app

The page now says You’re a tester for Phone Mouse by Easy Elm. Tap Download test app. Google Play opens Phone Mouse by Easy Elm (Early Access). Tap Install.

Install Phone Mouse by Easy Elm

Step 4: Stay in for 14 days

Keep the app installed and open it now and then. Tell us what you think at support@easyelm.com.
