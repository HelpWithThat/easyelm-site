Become a tester

Testers get the full app free while it’s in testing. It takes about two minutes.

Before you start, make sure you’re signed in to the same Google account that’s on your Android phone.

Step 1: Join the tester group

Tap the button below. You’ll see a page called Easy Elm Remote - Testers. Tap the blue Join group button near the top.

A box pops up that says Join Easy Elm Remote - Testers. You don’t need to change anything. Tap Join group again in the bottom corner of the box. You’ll see “Successfully joined the group.”

Join the tester group

Step 2: Become a tester

Tap the button below. The page says Easy Elm has invited you to a testing program. Tap the blue Become a tester button.

Become a tester

If it says “App not available,” check that you joined the group in Step 1 with the same Google account, then wait a few minutes and try again.

Step 3: Install the app

The page now says You are a tester. Tap the download it on Google Play link. On the Phone Mouse by Easy Elm page in Google Play, tap Install.

Install Phone Mouse by Easy Elm

Step 4: Stay in for 14 days

Keep the app installed and open it now and then. Tell us what you think at support@easyelm.com.
