Hold a name and talk.

What it does

A push-to-talk intercom for the phones in your home or workplace.

  • No account, no sign-up, and no ads.
  • Works over your own Wi-Fi, with no internet needed.
  • Voices are encrypted between your phones.
  • You hear people calling you even when your screen is off.

How it works, in 3 steps

  1. Put the app on each phone and give each one a name.
  2. Phones on the same Wi-Fi find each other on their own.
  3. Hold someone’s button to talk, and let go to stop. Or hold Everyone.

See it in action

Who it’s for

Anyone who wants to talk between phones in one home or workplace, on one Wi-Fi. Big buttons and clear lights make it easy for all ages.
