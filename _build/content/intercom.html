Hold a name and talk.

What it does

A push-to-talk intercom for the phones in your home or workplace.

  • No account, no sign-up, and no ads.
  • Works over Wi-Fi or mobile data. If the phones are on the same Wi-Fi, they can still talk even when the internet is down.
  • When you hold a button to talk, your voice is encrypted so only the other phones can hear it.
  • You hear people calling you even when your screen is off.

How it works, in 3 steps

  1. Put the app on each phone and give each one a name.
  2. Phones on the same Wi-Fi can find each other on their own. They can also talk from different places when each phone has internet (Wi-Fi or mobile data).
  3. Hold someone’s button to talk, and let go to stop. Or hold Everyone.

Who it’s for

Anyone who wants to talk between phones in a home or workplace — on the same Wi-Fi, or from different places over the internet. Big buttons and clear lights make it easy for all ages.
