Turn your Android phone or tablet into a wireless mouse, keyboard, mic (voice typing) and volume control for your computer, over Bluetooth.

Great for

  • Presenters: move through slides from anywhere in the room.
  • Couch use: control a computer hooked up to your TV without getting up.

Set up in 3 simple steps

  1. Open Easy Elm Remote on your phone and tap Make phone discoverable.
  2. On your Windows computer, open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Add device > Bluetooth, then pick your phone.
  3. Check that the codes match on both screens and confirm. That’s it. The app remembers your computer next time.

Questions

Does it work on iPhone?

No. Easy Elm Remote is for Android phones and tablets only.

Which computers does it work with?

Easy Elm Remote works with a Windows PC, laptop, or Android tablet. It hasn’t been tested with a Mac.

Do I need to install anything on my computer?

No. It connects over Bluetooth, so you don’t need a receiver or computer software.

Which keyboard layout does it use?

The keyboard uses the English (US) layout.

What happens after the free 7 days?

The mouse, keyboard, mic and volume buttons stay free forever. Pro features turn off unless you choose a Pro plan.
